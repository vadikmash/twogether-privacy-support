Twogether App

Privacy Policy & Support

What the app is

Twogether App is a set of mini-games for two adults sharing one phone. Version 1.0 includes an evening constructor that helps couples pick a place, an activity, and a twist for a date night.

Data we collect

We do not collect data from this app.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server backend for the app.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs that send data off-device.
  • Names, favorites, history, and settings stay on the device (local storage only).
  • Deleting the app deletes that local data. Settings also includes “Delete all data”.
